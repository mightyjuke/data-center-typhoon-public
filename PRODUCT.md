## Stack

## Users

Players discovering Compute Tycoon, existing players seeking support, and advertising crawlers verifying the developer.

## Product Purpose

Kevin Mok's public developer site for Compute Tycoon: Idle AI Game. Present the game, link to its live App Store and Google Play listings, make support and the existing privacy policy accessible, and serve the personalized app-ads.txt record.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The game expands a low-poly data center with GPUs, contracts, power, cooling, crew, research, and offline progression capped at 30 minutes. Optional purchases and rewarded ads exist. The iOS and Android store listings are live. Advertising verification requires an app-ads.txt file at the hostname root and the website linked from each store listing.

## Brand Commitments
